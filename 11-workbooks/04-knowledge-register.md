## Check conflicting information

Imagine a customer receives a quotation dated June, a price list dated September and an invoice dated November. Which source is authoritative? The answer depends on contract terms, valid effective dates and transaction status. Write that decision rule with the relevant record owner before allowing the assistant to answer automatically.

## Retire stale material

Do not leave old policies mixed with current instructions without clear superseded markers. If records must be retained for audit, keep them under a separate historical label or folder with controlled access. Test a known old question to make sure the current answer still wins.
