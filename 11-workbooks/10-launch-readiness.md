# Workbook: launch readiness and owner sign-off

Check every item against observable evidence. A checkbox means the business actually verified the behavior, not that a vendor promised it.

## Scope and records

- [ ] One first workflow and its end result are clearly defined.
- [ ] The existing manual process is documented.
- [ ] Every knowledge source has a named owner.
- [ ] Current prices, appointments or orders come from the real system of record.
- [ ] The task register stores status, next owner and completion evidence.

## Access and safety

- [ ] The connected account has only the necessary permissions.
- [ ] Forbidden actions were attempted in a test and did not succeed.
- [ ] Approval is enforced before sensitive operations.
- [ ] Untrusted documents cannot change the company's rules.
- [ ] A human can pause the workflow and revoke access.
- [ ] Applicable privacy, consent and retention duties have been reviewed.

## Quality and operations

- [ ] Normal, difficult and failure cases have been tested.
- [ ] The system reports uncertainty instead of inventing facts.
- [ ] Tool errors and uncertain outcomes have a defined path.
- [ ] Repeated requests will not silently duplicate high-impact actions.
- [ ] Staff know how to receive escalated cases.
- [ ] Usage costs, monitoring responsibilities and review dates are set.
