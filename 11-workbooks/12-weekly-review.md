# Workbook: the owner's weekly workforce review

Set aside a regular review with the process owner, the employee who handles escalations and whoever administers the connected systems. A workflow without maintenance will eventually drift away from the business's current information.

### Questions to discuss

Which cases did the assistant handle correctly? What assumptions caused problems? Which documents need updating? Which tool errors recurred? Are humans receiving the right handoff context? Are costs rising because of retries or a longer workflow? Is a new permission requested, and is it justified?
