## Immediate response sequence

Stop further harmful operations, prevent retries, preserve required records, determine the actual state in connected systems, inform the accountable owner, assess who is affected, and coordinate correction. Where sensitive data or regulated obligations are involved, involve the organization's appropriate specialists.

<details>
<summary>Why not simply press “retry”?</summary>

A timed-out action may have succeeded in the external system even if the automation did not receive confirmation. Repeating it could send another email, create a second order or make a duplicate financial entry. First inspect the actual record and its unique ID.

</details>
