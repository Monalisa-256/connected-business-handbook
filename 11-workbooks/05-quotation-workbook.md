### Practice: two similar items

A buyer requests two replacement pumps. The photograph looks like model P-210, but the price sheet includes P-210 and P-210B. The live inventory tool returns different quantities. Write the customer's reply **without selecting the model for them**.

<details>
<summary>One reasonable draft</summary>

Thank you for the photograph. Two products have similar labels, so I would like to confirm the complete model number before quoting. Could you send a clearer image of the full label or type the code? Once confirmed, we can check the current price, availability and delivery terms.

</details>

### Practice: a hidden fee

Supplier A charges 4,000 plus delivery 300. Supplier B charges 4,180 including delivery, but has a two-week lead time. A shallow comparison may select A based on base price. A correct comparison uses **4,300 versus 4,180** for the stated delivered totals and keeps lead-time preference separate.

**Sign-off:** The business owner verifies that the workflow never turns an unapproved draft into a binding quotation.
