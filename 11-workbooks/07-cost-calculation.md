### Calculation

**Cost per successful task = total monthly operating cost ÷ number of genuinely completed acceptable tasks.** If a workflow starts 1,000 times but completes only 400 requests correctly, use 400 as the denominator—not 1,000.

### Example with fictional numbers

Monthly cost is 600 money units, 750 tasks begin and 500 finish correctly. The cost per successful task is 600 ÷ 500 = **1.20 units**. If a revised workflow reduces total spend to 450 and successfully completes 600, the cost becomes **0.75 units**. Compare quality and human intervention, not cost alone.

### Decision discussion

Does this process save enough time to justify the setup and operating burden? Is the saved time actually redeployed? Did faster customer replies improve service? Were errors more expensive than expected? Are you paying for several tools that duplicate one another?
