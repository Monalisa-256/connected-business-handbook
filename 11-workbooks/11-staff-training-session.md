# Workbook: a staff training session that takes place in real workflows

The first training session should not be a presentation about futuristic technology. It should show employees what changes in their working day, how they can correct errors, and where responsibility remains human.

## Suggested session structure

**Part 1 — A familiar case.** Take a normal customer inquiry and show the existing manual steps. Ask staff where delays or repeated typing occur. **Part 2 — Demonstrate the proposed workflow.** Show the intake record, source lookup, draft, approval and real system result. **Part 3 — Show a failure deliberately.** Disable a test connection or use an ambiguous sample. Demonstrate the expected escalation and task register.

**Part 4 — Practice review.** Give two groups a draft quotation. One has a wrong quantity; the other has missing delivery terms. Ask them to identify the problems before approval. **Part 5 — Define ownership.** Agree who reviews exceptions, who corrects knowledge and who can stop the system.

## Staff questions to answer

- Is this replacing a task, changing a role, or creating a new approval responsibility?
- How does an employee correct a wrong answer?
- Where are customer messages and attachments stored?
- How are unauthorized requests handled?
- Who handles an angry customer who asks for a person?
- What happens during a platform outage?
- Can employees report unsafe behavior without penalty?
