# Thirty digital roles: a practical reference library

A role should be defined by the work it owns, not by a grand job title. Some businesses will combine several roles in one assistant; others will use the same role across multiple departments. The following is a catalog of useful responsibilities.

## Customer and commercial work

| Role | Job it owns | Records or capabilities required | Human boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake clerk | Registers customer requests with source and status | Messages, forms, task register | No commitments |
| Customer FAQ desk | Explains approved policies | Current FAQ and policy library | Escalates exceptions |
| Appointment receptionist | Finds and offers available times | Live calendar, voice or text | No fictional bookings |
| Lead registrar | Deduplicates and records prospects | CRM search and create | Does not approve discounts |
| Quotation preparer | Calculates and drafts proposals from verified terms | Catalog, rate card, arithmetic | Commercial approval |
| Order-status desk | Explains order and delivery state | Order and courier lookup | Does not alter courier records |
| Returns coordinator | Gathers order and complaint evidence | Order data, return policy | Refund authorization separate |
| Account follow-up assistant | Prepares agreed follow-ups | CRM stage and communication history | Message approval and contact rules |
| Survey analyst | Groups customer feedback themes | Consented survey records | Distinguishes opinion from fact |
| Client onboarding coordinator | Creates approved welcome tasks | CRM, checklist, calendar | Access grants controlled by staff |

## Documents, operations and finance

| Role | Job it owns | Records or capabilities required | Human boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Document classifier | Sorts incoming files | File metadata and text extraction | No silent deletion |
| Invoice extractor | Reads invoice fields | OCR or document processing | No payment authority |
| Purchase-order matcher | Compares approved purchase records | PO and goods receipt | Handles mismatch as exception |
| Supplier inquiry desk | Answers routine supplier questions | Contracts and purchase-order status | No bank-detail changes |
| Inventory question desk | Checks quantities and locations | Live stock system | No unapproved reservations |
| Dispatch coordinator | Tracks shipments and exceptions | Carrier and order records | Claims confirmed by source |
| Maintenance ticket clerk | Organizes equipment observations | Asset register, staff reports | Safety decision with qualified staff |
| Expense report preparer | Organizes receipts and categories | Expense policy and accounting categories | Tax and audit decisions with finance |
| Scheduling assistant | Proposes staff or room schedules | Calendar and availability | Labor and safety rules enforced |
| Procurement comparison writer | Summarizes offers and exclusions | Original quotations, catalog | Purchase decision with owner |

## Knowledge, content and management

| Role | Job it owns | Records or capabilities required | Human boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Researcher | Finds sourced public information | Search, date checks, citations | No unverified market claims |
| Brief writer | Turns findings into readable decision notes | Source material, format requirements | Owner decides strategy |
| Brand copywriter | Drafts campaign language | Brand guide, product facts | Publishing approval |
| Visual concept maker | Produces ideas and illustrations | Approved assets, design tools | Product depiction checked |
| Video script editor | Drafts storyboards and narration | Approved claims and audience brief | Footage rights and final review |
| Meeting summarizer | Records decisions and next actions | Consented notes, task register | Attendees verify sensitive decisions |
| Procedure librarian | Detects missing or stale guides | Document inventory, ownership data | Policy owner approves revisions |
| Data narrator | Explains verified metrics | Reporting tables, calculation tool | Does not fabricate numbers |
| Quality checker | Tests outputs against sources and rules | Original input and answer key | Not a substitute for accountable approval |
| Work coordinator | Assigns and tracks specialist tasks | Task register, routing rules | Cannot grant itself permissions |

## How to write a good role description

For every role, fill in **purpose, input, approved sources, allowed actions, prohibited actions, output, handoff, acceptance criteria and human owner**. Avoid vague instructions like "be proactive and run the business." A useful role description says "Prepare a draft quotation from the approved price list and record missing fields; do not send or apply discounts."

## When roles should be split

Split one role into two when the tasks need different permissions, operating times, types of evidence, or quality checks. For example, an invoice extractor and a payment approver must remain separate responsibilities. A marketing copywriter and a final publisher may also need separation. Do not split roles just to make a diagram look more impressive.

## When roles should be combined

A very small business may combine intake, FAQ and task tracking in one restricted assistant. Keep the responsibilities explicit and use deterministic automation for the record changes. If the combined role repeatedly makes mistakes or requires conflicting data access, split it later.
