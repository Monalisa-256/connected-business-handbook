# Template: working instructions and handoff note

Use this when one specialist passes a job to another. The goal is to eliminate repeated questions and lost context.

## Reusable working instructions

> **Your job:** [Specific task.]  
> **Your authorized information:** [Named sources.]  
> **Your permitted actions:** [What connected tools can actually do.]  
> **Your limits:** [Actions requiring human approval and prohibited actions.]  
> **Method:** Confirm the request, use approved facts, complete the permitted steps, verify tool results, and record unresolved issues.  
> **If anything is missing:** Do not guess. State what is missing and who should provide it.  
> **Result:** Produce the output structure below.

## Worked handoff example

| Field | Sample entry |
|---|---|
| Task ID | Q-104 |
| Original request | Quote 20 units of Product B for Friday delivery |
| Who started it | Customer inquiry to sales inbox |
| Priority | Normal |
| Verified facts | Product B is listed; customer requested quantity 20 |
| Original records | Current catalog item B22 |
| Actions already completed | Identified item and prepared request summary |
| Confirmation received | None; no external action taken |
| What remains unknown | Stock and delivery capacity |
| Required human approval | Discount or special delivery terms |
| Next owner | Sales specialist, then Operations |
| Follow-up | Before a price or delivery promise is sent |

A handoff that does not distinguish **verified** from **unverified** information is incomplete.

## Handoff example: from Sales to Operations

> **Task:** QUOTE-2026-041  
> **Received from:** Sales inbox, inquiry #message-418  
> **Customer request:** 12 units of product P, delivery by next Thursday  
> **Verified product:** SKU-P, catalog revision 4  
> **Verified unit price:** 34 currency units, price list dated 10 November 2026  
> **Unverified fact:** Available quantity at the requested delivery date  
> **Work completed:** Customer record located; duplicate inquiry check clear; quote draft prepared but not sent  
> **Operations request:** Confirm stock and delivery feasibility with authoritative system records  
> **Do not do:** Do not promise availability or send the quotation  
> **Next owner:** Operations  
> **Completion evidence expected:** Stock record reference and delivery commitment or explicit inability to verify

A good handoff is small enough to scan and complete enough to avoid asking the customer for information already supplied. Use identifiers and source references rather than pasting full confidential messages into every handoff.
