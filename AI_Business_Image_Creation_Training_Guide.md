## LESSON 1: UNDERSTANDING AI IMAGE GENERATION

AI image generation is the process of using artificial intelligence to create images from written instructions, existing pictures, or a combination of both.

The instructions given to AI are called **prompts**.

For example:

A weak prompt:

> "Create a picture of a perfume."

A stronger prompt:

> "Create a professional commercial photograph of an elegant perfume bottle standing on a polished marble surface, with soft golden lighting, a luxury beige background, realistic reflections, natural shadows and a clean advertising composition. Vertical 4:5 aspect ratio."

The second prompt gives the AI much more direction.

**Important principle:** AI produces the initial image, but a professional creator must inspect, correct, improve and prepare it for its intended purpose.

A beautiful image is not necessarily a successful advertisement. A successful business image must attract attention, communicate the correct message, build trust and encourage a customer to take action.

## LESSON 3: PLANNING BEFORE GENERATING

Before opening an AI image generator, define what you want to achieve.

Ask yourself these seven questions:

1. What business is this image for?
2. Which product or service am I promoting?
3. Who is the target customer?
4. Where will the image be published?
5. What feeling should the image create?
6. What colors represent the business?
7. What action should the customer take?

For example:

Business: Clothing shop.

Product: Women's casual dresses.

Target customers: Young adult women.

Platform: Instagram and WhatsApp.

Desired feeling: Modern, fashionable and trustworthy.

Brand colors: Black, cream and gold.

Customer action: Message the shop to order.

Once these details are clear, writing a strong prompt becomes much easier.

**Never begin by generating random pictures and hoping one will suit the business.**

## LESSON 4: IMAGE RATIOS AND DIMENSIONS

This is one of the most important technical lessons.

An **aspect ratio** is the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image.

For example:

1:1 means the width and height are equal.

4:5 means the image is slightly taller than it is wide.

9:16 means the image is much taller than it is wide.

16:9 means the image is wider than it is tall.

### Important ratios to memorize

| Ratio | Example size | Common use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 px | Square posts and product graphics |
| 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 px | Instagram and Facebook feed advertisements |
| 3:4 | 1080 × 1440 px | Portrait photography and supported Instagram feed photos |
| 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 px | Stories, WhatsApp Status and vertical content |
| 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 px | Wide advertising graphics and video thumbnails |
| 1.91:1 | 1080 × 566 px | Landscape social media images |
| A4 | About 2480 × 3508 px | A4 print at 300 pixels per inch |

These are useful creation sizes, not universal mandatory dimensions for every platform.

### How to choose the correct ratio

If creating an Instagram feed advertisement, begin with 4:5.

If creating a WhatsApp Status advertisement, begin with 9:16.

If creating a square product catalog image, use 1:1.

If creating a wide presentation graphic, use 16:9.

If creating a printed flyer, use the actual paper dimensions requested by the printing shop.

**Important:** Instagram also supports 3:4 feed photographs, but a 4:5 design remains a practical choice for many feed advertising and scheduling workflows.

### Understand ratio versus resolution

Ratio describes the shape.

Resolution describes the number of pixels.

For example, 400 × 500 and 1080 × 1350 are both 4:5 images, but the second contains more pixels.

More pixels can provide greater detail when the underlying image is genuinely high quality.

### How to request a ratio

Tell the AI:

> "Generate this advertisement in a vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, with the product centered and sufficient room for a headline."

Also choose the matching ratio in the tool's settings if that option is provided.

Some tools use specific commands. For example, Midjourney supports `--ar 4:5`. This command is not universal across AI tools.

**Teaching rule:** Decide the final ratio before generating whenever possible.

## LESSON 5: HOW TO WRITE STRONG AI PROMPTS

A professional image prompt should contain the following information:

1. **Task:** What the AI should create.
2. **Subject:** The main object or person.
3. **Environment:** Where the subject appears.
4. **Composition:** Where everything should be positioned.
5. **Lighting:** How the scene should be illuminated.
6. **Style:** The visual appearance.
7. **Colors:** The desired color palette.
8. **Realism:** How natural or stylized it should look.
9. **Restrictions:** What must not appear or change.
10. **Output:** Ratio, orientation and intended platform.

### The master prompt formula

**TASK + SUBJECT + ENVIRONMENT + COMPOSITION + LIGHTING + STYLE + COLORS + REALISM + RESTRICTIONS + OUTPUT**

You do not need all ten elements in every prompt, but they form a useful checklist.

### Complete example

> Create a premium commercial product photograph for a cosmetics advertisement.
>
> The main subject is a luxury body lotion bottle on a clean cream-colored pedestal.
>
> Place the bottle prominently in the center of the frame, with realistic contact shadows and a softly blurred background.
>
> Use warm natural studio lighting, accurate highlights, realistic surface texture and a sophisticated beige-and-gold color palette.
>
> Make the image look like genuine commercial photography, not a cartoon or plastic-looking AI rendering.
>
> Keep the composition clean, preserve realistic product proportions, and leave some empty space above the bottle for a headline.
>
> No random text, extra bottles, floating objects or unnecessary decorations.
>
> Output a high-quality vertical image in 4:5 aspect ratio.

### What makes this prompt effective?

It explains the subject, setting, lighting, colors, positioning, quality expectations and restrictions.

This reduces guesswork for the AI.

However, a prompt cannot guarantee perfect accuracy. Always inspect the output.

## LESSON 6: HUMANIZATION OF AI IMAGES

Humanization means improving AI-generated images so they feel natural, believable, relatable and emotionally convincing.

Sometimes AI-generated images look artificial because people have unusually smooth skin, unnatural expressions, incorrect hands or unrealistic body proportions.

A professional business image should not look unnecessarily fake.

### A. Humanizing faces

For realistic people, request:

- Natural skin texture.
- Realistic facial proportions.
- Normal facial expressions.
- Authentic skin tones.
- Realistic eyes and teeth.
- Natural hair texture.
- Believable lighting.
- Normal body proportions.

Avoid unnecessarily perfect faces, excessively glossy skin, unnatural smiles and exaggerated facial features.

### Humanization prompt

> "Improve the realism of the person in this image. Preserve their identity and facial features. Maintain natural skin texture, believable facial proportions, authentic skin tone, realistic eyes, natural hair and a relaxed expression. Reduce excessive skin smoothing, artificial shine and exaggerated beauty effects. Keep the result professionally photographed but genuinely human."

When editing a real person's photograph, preserve their identity and do not make unwanted changes to their appearance.

### B. Humanizing poses

A subject may look artificial because of an unnatural pose.

Ask for:

> "Natural body posture, realistic weight distribution, relaxed shoulders, believable hand positioning and authentic interaction with the environment."

Always inspect fingers, hands, arms and clothing.

### C. Humanizing the environment

Realistic surroundings help an image appear believable.

For example, a boutique advertisement could contain a genuine-looking clothing rack, natural indoor lighting and normal shop details.

Avoid floating products, impossible reflections, unrealistic shadows and unnecessarily perfect backgrounds.

### D. Humanizing business messages

Humanization also applies to advertising text.

Robotic message:

> "Experience our unparalleled assortment of superior garments."

Humanized message:

> "Find outfits you'll love wearing every day."

The second is easier to understand and more relatable.

**Key lesson:** Humanization does not mean deliberately creating flaws. It means removing artificial-looking effects while preserving authenticity.

## LESSON 7: IMAGE IMPROVEMENT AND ENHANCEMENT

After generating an image, do not immediately publish it.

Inspect and improve it systematically.

### Step 1: Correct the composition

Composition is the arrangement of objects in the image.

Check whether:

- The main subject is easy to notice.
- Nothing important is accidentally cut off.
- The image is balanced.
- There is sufficient space for text.
- Distracting objects have been removed.

Improvement prompt:

> "Improve the composition by making the main product more prominent, balancing the empty space and removing unnecessary background distractions. Keep the original subject and its details unchanged."

### Step 2: Improve lighting

Good lighting helps show shape, texture and product quality.

Useful lighting instructions include:

**Soft studio lighting:** Suitable for cosmetics, skincare and catalog photography.

**Natural daylight:** Suitable for clothing, food and lifestyle images.

**Warm lighting:** Suitable for restaurants, hotels and welcoming environments.

**Directional lighting:** Suitable for dramatic, high-end promotional photography.

Example:

> "Improve the lighting using soft, balanced studio illumination, realistic shadows and controlled highlights. Make the product clearly visible without overexposure."

### Step 3: Improve color balance

Good colors must appear intentional, consistent and attractive.

Check:

- Is the image too yellow?
- Is the image too blue?
- Are the colors dull?
- Is the saturation excessive?
- Does the product still have its real color?

Example:

> "Adjust white balance, brightness and color contrast to produce natural, attractive colors. Maintain the actual colors of the product and avoid oversaturation."

### Step 4: Improve sharpness

Sharpness makes edges and important details appear clearer.

Example:

> "Improve the apparent clarity of the product, reduce unwanted noise, preserve natural textures and apply subtle sharpening without artificial halos."

Be careful: excessive sharpening can create rough edges and unnatural textures.

Also remember that sharpening cannot truly recover every detail missing from a blurry original.

### Step 5: Correct AI mistakes

Zoom into the image.

Look for:

- Incorrect fingers.
- Distorted faces.
- Misspelled writing.
- Incorrect logos.
- Unnatural shadows.
- Duplicate objects.
- Strange reflections.
- Distorted packaging.
- Missing product details.

Use a localized editing tool whenever possible.

For example:

> "Correct only the distorted hand while keeping the person's face, outfit, body position, lighting and background unchanged."

Making one controlled edit at a time often works better than regenerating the entire image repeatedly.

## LESSON 8: ANTI-FADING — HOW TO KEEP IMAGES CLEAR AND VIBRANT

There are two different problems commonly described as fading.

### A. Digital images looking faded or washed out

A digital image may appear faded because of low contrast, incorrect exposure, excessive brightness, poor color balance, inappropriate filters, color-profile problems or image compression.

### How to reduce a washed-out appearance

1. Correct the white balance.
2. Adjust exposure so that important details remain visible.
3. Improve midtone contrast.
4. Restore natural color depth.
5. Avoid excessive gray or white overlays.
6. Preserve shadow detail.
7. Prevent bright areas from losing detail.
8. Avoid excessive saturation.
9. Export using appropriate color settings.

### Digital anti-fading prompt

> "Improve this image's color clarity and tonal balance. Restore rich but natural colors, balanced midtone contrast, clean whites, realistic blacks and detailed shadows. Remove the washed-out gray appearance without making the image oversaturated. Preserve highlights, natural skin tones and authentic product colors."

This is a useful improvement prompt when an image looks dull.

However, **there is no universal AI phrase that permanently prevents an image from fading**. Actual color appearance also depends on the file, screen, software and export settings.

### Understanding sRGB

sRGB is a widely used color space for websites and digital images.

For ordinary online publishing, using sRGB helps promote consistent color across browsers and consumer devices.

Do not assume every screen will display exactly identical colors.

### B. Physical fading of printed images

Printed posters, photographs and advertisements can fade because of sunlight, ultraviolet exposure, heat, humidity, unsuitable paper and ink quality.

To improve print longevity:

- Use suitable, quality inks and printing materials.
- Ask the printer about fade-resistant or pigment-based options.
- Choose suitable photo paper or durable printing media.
- Avoid unnecessary direct sunlight.
- Consider appropriate UV-protective finishes for exposed displays.
- Store prints in dry, suitable conditions.

**Important:** An AI prompt cannot make ordinary printing ink physically fade-proof. Print durability depends on real materials and environmental conditions.

## LESSON 9: PROFESSIONAL COLOR GRADING

Color grading is the process of adjusting the overall visual mood of an image.

Different businesses require different visual styles.

### Luxury products

Colors: Black, gold, cream, deep brown.

Mood: Elegant and sophisticated.

Prompt terms: Controlled highlights, rich tones, subtle reflections, premium studio lighting.

### Beauty products

Colors: Soft pink, beige, cream, pastel tones.

Mood: Clean, gentle and welcoming.

Prompt terms: Soft diffused light, delicate shadows, natural skin texture.

### Food businesses

Colors: Warm amber, appetizing natural food colors.

Mood: Fresh, inviting and delicious.

Prompt terms: Warm daylight, realistic food texture, appetizing presentation, natural shadows.

### Technology businesses

Colors: Navy blue, white, charcoal and selected bright accents.

Mood: Modern, clean and innovative.

Prompt terms: Crisp lighting, geometric composition, precise details, contemporary commercial photography.

### Fashion businesses

Colors: Depend on brand identity.

Mood: Modern, stylish and authentic.

Prompt terms: Editorial fashion photography, natural posing, realistic fabric texture, professional styling.

Remember that color recommendations are creative starting points, not compulsory rules.

Brand consistency is more important than copying popular color schemes.

## LESSON 10: CREATING A BUSINESS BRAND IDENTITY

Good business imagery must look consistent.

A brand identity includes:

1. Business name.
2. Logo.
3. Main colors.
4. Secondary colors.
5. Typography or fonts.
6. Photography style.
7. General design personality.

Example brand system:

Business: A premium fashion boutique.

Main color: Deep black.

Secondary color: Warm cream.

Accent color: Muted gold.

Photography style: Natural studio portraits.

Headline style: Elegant, simple and bold.

Every new advertisement should generally follow this style.

### Brand consistency prompt

> "Create a new advertisement that follows our existing brand identity. Use the supplied logo and reference materials. Maintain our black, cream and muted-gold palette, elegant composition, natural commercial photography and clean visual hierarchy. Do not redesign our logo or alter the brand name."

For repeatable work, save a brand template or brand kit.

**Professional rule:** Generate image concepts with AI, but use the original approved logo file rather than relying on AI to redraw it perfectly.

## LESSON 11: TEXT PLACEMENT AND TYPOGRAPHY

A professional advertisement normally contains a clear hierarchy.

### Example hierarchy

**Headline:** What should people notice first?

**Main image:** What is being advertised?

**Supporting information:** Why should customers care?

**Call to action:** What should the customer do next?

Example:

Headline: NEW ARRIVALS

Supporting information: Stylish everyday dresses.

Call to action: Message us to order.

Contact information: Use the business's real contact details.

### Typography rules

- Use one or two complementary fonts.
- Make the headline easy to read.
- Maintain sufficient contrast between text and background.
- Avoid placing small text over complicated photographs.
- Use consistent margins.
- Keep the call to action visible.
- Leave room around important text.

When exact prices, phone numbers, addresses or legal wording matter, it is often safer to add them as editable text inside Canva.

AI-generated text inside images can still contain mistakes.

## LESSON 13: WORKING WITH REAL PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHS

When selling a real product, accuracy is extremely important.

Suppose a customer is buying a particular handbag.

AI might change the:

- Number of zippers.
- Brand logo.
- Handle shape.
- Fabric texture.
- Stitching.
- Color.
- Size or proportions.

The result may look beautiful but no longer represent the actual item.

### Preservation prompt

> "Use the uploaded photograph as the primary product reference. Preserve its exact shape, actual color, visible logo, hardware, stitching, texture and proportions. Improve only the background, lighting, positioning and natural shadows. Do not invent or change product features."

Even this instruction does not guarantee accuracy.

For strict product fidelity, keep the original product photograph as a separate foreground layer and use AI primarily to create or improve the background.

Always compare the output with the original.

- No duplicated products.

**Version 1 — Minimalist**

Clean background, soft lighting, very few decorative elements.

Elegant color palette, controlled reflections and sophisticated composition.

## LESSON 16: EXPORT QUALITY AND PREVENTING BLUR

A good image can become poor after incorrect exporting.

### PNG

Useful for:

- Posters containing text.
- Graphics with sharp edges.
- Logos and transparent backgrounds where supported.

PNG generally preserves image detail without the additional lossy compression used in standard JPG files.

### JPG or JPEG

Useful for:

- Product photographs.
- Social media photography.
- Smaller image file sizes.

Choose high-quality export settings when available.

Repeatedly saving a compressed JPG may introduce additional artifacts.

### PDF Print

Useful for:

- Printed flyers.
- Brochures.
- Professional posters.
- Documents intended for a printing business.

A PDF Print export can preserve editable-layout-derived text and graphics more effectively than a small raster image.

### PPI and DPI

PPI means pixels per inch in a digital image.

DPI means dots per inch in printing equipment.

For many close-view commercial prints, approximately 300 pixels per inch at the final printed size is a useful target.

Large banners may use different requirements because of their viewing distance.

The printing shop should confirm its preferred resolution, dimensions, bleed and color specifications.

### RGB and CMYK

RGB is commonly used for screens.

CMYK refers to cyan, magenta, yellow and black, the inks used in conventional process printing.

Do not blindly convert every image to CMYK.

Ask your printer whether the final file should be RGB or CMYK and which color profile they require.

### Sharing through WhatsApp

WhatsApp may compress images.

When sending an important image, choose HD where available.

For transferring an original file to a designer or printer, sending it as a document/file may help avoid ordinary photo-sharing compression.

WhatsApp Status may still reduce image quality.

**Important rule:** Preserve the original high-quality file and do not use a screenshot as the master copy.

## LESSON 18: HOW TO FIX COMMON AI IMAGE PROBLEMS

**Problem: The image looks too artificial.**

Solution: Request natural textures, believable lighting, realistic proportions and less aggressive retouching.

**Problem: The colors are dull.**

Solution: Correct the white balance, exposure and midtone contrast. Increase saturation only when necessary.

**Problem: The face looks plastic.**

Solution: Ask for natural pores, realistic skin texture, soft lighting and reduced beauty effects.

**Problem: The background is too busy.**

Solution: Request fewer objects, softer background detail and stronger subject separation.

**Problem: The product looks different from the original.**

Solution: Use the original product image as a preserved layer. Regenerate or edit only the surroundings when accuracy matters.

**Problem: The image is blurry.**

Solution: Check the source image and export resolution. Consider an appropriate upscale or controlled sharpening, but remember that AI may invent details.

**Problem: The poster looks unprofessional.**

Solution: Simplify the layout, improve spacing, use fewer fonts and establish one clear headline.

**Problem: The AI changes the logo.**

Solution: Use the original logo file as an overlay in Canva.

**Problem: The text is misspelled.**

Solution: Replace it with accurately typed, editable text.

**Problem: The image looks good in the editor but bad after posting.**

Solution: Check ratio, export quality, color profile, platform compression and cropping.

## LESSON 19: BRAND SAFETY AND PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY

Business images must also be trustworthy.

Follow these rules:

1. Do not create misleading product details.
2. Do not advertise an AI-invented feature as if the product really has it.
3. Use correct prices and company information.
4. Do not fabricate customer testimonials or reviews.
5. Do not use another company's protected logo without authorization.
6. Get appropriate permission before using identifiable real people's photographs commercially.
7. Check the image tool's commercial-use terms.
8. Be transparent when an AI-generated visual could otherwise mislead customers about real people, products or events.

AI is useful for creativity, but the business remains responsible for what it publishes.

## LESSON 21: IMPORTANT PROFESSIONAL VOCABULARY

**Prompt:** Written instruction given to AI.

**Prompt engineering:** Structuring and refining instructions for better results.

**Aspect ratio:** The relationship between image width and height.

**Resolution:** Image dimensions measured in pixels.

**Humanization:** Making generated content feel natural, authentic and relatable.

**Enhancement:** Improving an image's appearance or quality.

**Upscaling:** Increasing image dimensions, sometimes using AI to estimate additional detail.

**Retouching:** Correcting selected visual imperfections.

**Composition:** The arrangement of subjects and other visual elements.

**Color grading:** Adjusting colors and tones to establish a particular visual appearance.

**Exposure:** The overall lightness or darkness of an image.

**Contrast:** The difference between lighter and darker tones.

**Saturation:** The intensity of colors.

**White balance:** Adjustment that helps neutral colors appear neutral under different lighting.

**Negative prompt:** An instruction identifying elements or qualities to avoid.

**Reference image:** An existing image used to guide the generation or editing process.

**Inpainting:** Editing or replacing a selected region within an image.

**Outpainting:** Extending an image beyond its original boundaries.

**Background removal:** Isolating a subject from its surroundings.

**Typography:** The selection and arrangement of text.

**Visual hierarchy:** The order in which viewers notice important design elements.

**Call to action (CTA):** Text encouraging a customer to act, such as "Shop Now."

**Color profile:** Information that defines how image colors should be interpreted.

**Compression:** Reducing file size, sometimes at the expense of image quality.

**Brand consistency:** Maintaining a recognizable visual identity across business materials.

# FINAL LESSON: THE PROFESSIONAL AI IMAGE WORKFLOW

Memorize this sequence:

**1. PLAN** — Understand the business and image purpose.

**2. SELECT** — Choose the most suitable AI tool.

**3. SIZE** — Determine the correct aspect ratio and dimensions.

**4. PROMPT** — Write clear, specific image instructions.

**5. GENERATE** — Create several suitable options.

**6. INSPECT** — Check realism, accuracy and composition.

**7. HUMANIZE** — Correct artificial-looking details.

**8. ENHANCE** — Improve lighting, colors and clarity.

**9. PROTECT ACCURACY** — Preserve actual product and brand details.

**10. BRAND** — Add logos, fonts, headlines and contact information.

**11. EXPORT** — Choose the right file format, quality and color settings.

**12. VERIFY** — Check the final image before publishing.

**13. PUBLISH** — Share it on the intended platform.

**14. EVALUATE** — Review how customers respond and improve future designs.

## GOLDEN RULE

A professional AI image is not defined by how many effects it contains.

It is defined by how clearly it communicates, how believable it looks, how faithfully it represents the business and how effectively it serves its purpose.

**Good prompting creates a strong starting point. Good judgment and careful editing create professional results.**
