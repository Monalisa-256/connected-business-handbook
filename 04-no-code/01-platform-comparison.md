# Comparing no-code workforce platforms

No single platform does everything equally well. A visual workflow builder is especially good at predictable office routines. A dedicated agent-workforce platform is designed for handing work between specialists. A voice product is optimized for spoken conversations. An enterprise platform may offer stronger administrative controls.

The descriptions below are based on official documentation, **not** an independent performance ranking. Features and plan limits change; confirm them before purchase.

| Product | Best starting situation | Strength | Something to investigate |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Relevance AI](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/core-concepts/workforces) | You want a named team of specialist agents | Visual canvas, handoffs, specialized tools | Permission model, integrations, credits and monitoring |
| [Make](https://www.make.com/) | Existing apps need to work together | Visual scenarios, triggers, app connections and agent steps | Mapping, retries, usage and scenario complexity |
| [Microsoft Copilot Studio](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/) | Business uses Microsoft 365 and Power Platform | Organizational knowledge, tools, governance | Licensing, administration, deployment channels |
| [Google Workspace Studio](https://workspace.google.com/studio/) | Business lives in Gmail, Drive, Sheets and Calendar | Workspace-centered visual flows | Exact triggers, permissions and app support |
| [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) | Straightforward event-based tasks | Wide range of app automations | Task usage, approval steps, AI features currently offered |
| [Voiceflow](https://www.voiceflow.com/) | Customer chat or phone is central | Conversation design, knowledge, testing, human handoff | Phone provider, call cost, language coverage |
| [ElevenLabs ElevenAgents](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/overview) | Natural spoken conversation is central | Voice experience and agent workflows | Call cost, language accuracy, integrations |
| [Botpress](https://botpress.com/) | Customer support across chat channels | Customer-facing agent tools and integrations | Supported channels and escalation setup |

## Choose by the work, not the advertising

### Start with Relevance AI when...

You want the receptionist to pass a request to Sales, Sales to consult Operations, and a Reviewer to check the result. The [Workforces documentation](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/core-concepts/workforces) describes a visual canvas for this kind of cooperation.

### Start with Make when...

Your job sounds like "When a new email arrives, extract the order number, look up a record, create a task, and notify staff." A visual sequence may be more understandable and reliable than several agents talking to each other. [Make AI Agents](https://www.make.com/) can be used within this broader automation approach.

### Start with Microsoft when...

The company already governs staff, files, mail, and workflows through Microsoft 365. Copilot Studio and Power Automate can support a controlled organization-wide rollout. [Microsoft MCP guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/agent-extend-action-mcp) is relevant if you later need that connection standard.

### Start with Google when...

The office already uses Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Sheets. [Google Workspace Studio](https://workspace.google.com/studio/) provides a no-code workflow approach. Check whether your particular automation requires an available Workspace step or additional services.

### Start with Voiceflow or ElevenLabs when...

The main job happens on a telephone or in spoken conversation. Begin with one call type and a clear transfer to a person; integrate the other departments later.

## Questions to ask before paying

- Can the system finish the exact workflow you have written down?
- Can you inspect a run and see which step failed?
- Can you limit a connection to read-only access?
- Does the product support approval before external messages or record changes?
- Is the service available and supported in your country?
- How are voice minutes, document pages, credits, and app actions billed?
- What happens if a staff member leaves or the owner changes?
- Can you export your records and move to another service?

See [vendor questions](../07-templates/07-vendor-questions.md).

## What a live demonstration should prove

A vendor may show an attractive interface that drafts a friendly answer. Ask them to demonstrate a task that includes an actual external system: retrieve a verified record, handle an unavailable field, request approval, perform an allowed action, show a system receipt and display the completed task. Then ask the demonstrator to try a prohibited operation. A useful platform makes both successful work and denied work observable.

### Separate three selection priorities

**Workforce teamwork:** Visual specialists and handoffs may matter most. **Cross-app operations:** Reliable triggers, mapping, retries and app permissions may matter most. **Customer channels:** Voice quality, live transfer, messaging policy and language support may matter most. The best platform for one priority may not be the best for the others.

A reasonable owner can begin with the tools already in the company, particularly if they provide approved identity controls and support. Moving to another vendor should be justified by a tested capability or economics advantage, not by a more impressive product label.
