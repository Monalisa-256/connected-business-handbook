# Official sources and further reading

## How these sources were used

- **Definitions** of MCP, tools, resources and prompts come from the protocol documents.
- **Platform features** are based on the relevant platform's own documentation. They are subject to plan and product changes.
- **Model selection advice** is editorial guidance grounded in the kinds of capabilities providers document, not a benchmark-based ranking.
- **Business scenarios, cost examples and checklists** are original illustrations prepared for this handbook; figures are fictional and do not represent vendor performance.
- **Security recommendations** draw on OWASP, NIST and the practical principle of keeping consequential actions under verifiable control.

When a plan, product label or available model differs from a description in the handbook, prioritize the current official vendor documentation and test the feature before relying on it.
